# 贴纸手账

Project adaptation of TanShilongMario/visual-memory-translator-SKILL, style_mode=layered_sticker_reassembly. Original skill, selected references and MIT license are preserved in upstream/. This self-contained app preset uses photo input, a single final artwork, and no holiday overlay or style-preview grid.

## Source analysis

Read the actual photograph: main subject, pose, clothing, accessories, foreground, middle ground, distant background and source colors. Select the memory worth preserving. Keep people and objects photographically recognizable; never redraw the person as a cartoon, change their identity or beautify their face. Do not add companions, accessories, landmarks or objects missing from the source.

## Three-layer maximum

Separate at most three truthful photographic fragments: primary subject, a middle-ground fragment and a distant-background fragment. Use two layers if the photograph does not contain three meaningful layers. Keep the source's spatial relationships readable even while changing scale. The main subject is largest; secondary environment is smaller; distant environment is smallest or faintest. Keep a partial body at its honest original crop; do not invent hidden hands, legs or occluded scenery.

Give each fragment a clean warm-white sticker edge, visually consistent and about 2–5% of that sticker's short edge. Use no shadow, or one very faint, short soft shadow. Create distinct separated layers with restrained partial overlap; do not fuse the whole source into one irregular photo sticker and do not repeat the same person across layers.

## Memory-page layout

Make a single flat vertical 3:5 artwork on clean warm off-white art paper with only subtle fibers and no aging. The complete sticker group occupies roughly 25–40% of the canvas area and at least 50% remains open paper. Group the fragments with an intentional asymmetric balance and a clear focal subject. Keep the original visual narrative understandable, while letting the page breathe.

Use one very short poetic title (2–5 words, or a supplied short Chinese title) in quiet serif or restrained handwriting, tucked into a clear paper margin and aligned to the group. If exact wording is supplied, preserve it. No unrelated slogan, long text, fake time/location/serial, signature or decorative metadata. Do not turn the composition into a literal screenshot of an app.

## Avoid

No sticker packs, emoji faces, colorful outlines, decorative tape, paper scraps, torn edges, thick drop shadows, 3D scrapbook effects, repeated subjects, more than three photo layers, collage clutter, full-page landscape repainting or artificially completed hidden content. Render only the finished paper artwork; no desk, hands holding it, external frame, multi-panel comparison or original-photo inset.

## Visual check

Recognizable source person/object, true original pose and crop, distinct clean-edged layers, source-related environmental fragments, generous whitespace, restrained short title and no unrelated decoration.
