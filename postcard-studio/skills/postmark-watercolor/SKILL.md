# 水彩邮记

Project adaptation of pamler1004/postmark, watercolor mode. Original skill and MIT license are preserved in upstream/. This file is the self-contained image prompt used by the app; no external files or tools are required during image generation.

## Source fidelity

Read the supplied photograph first. Preserve its main subject, recognizable architecture, clothing, pose, horizon, lighting mood, meaningful signs, and spatial relationships. Repaint the photograph into watercolor; do not paste an unmodified photograph inside a decorative border. Do not substitute a famous landmark, invent a location, alter cultural identity, or add unrelated people.

## Watercolor medium

Use soft transparent washes, wet-on-wet blending, gentle color bleeds and blooms, visible brush strokes, delicate pencil or fine ink accents on key contours. Let the paper shine through sky, water and distant shapes. Keep convincing source-derived color relationships, luminous edges and atmospheric depth. Use neutral watercolor paper with fine fibers, matte absorption grain and slight ink-density variation throughout the scene. No automatic sepia, yellowing, brown cast, grime, heavy aging, plastic surfaces or photographic filter effect.

## Postcard composition

Produce one flat, finished vertical 3:5 artwork, directly facing the viewer. Build a watercolor commemorative-stamp composition inside the canvas, with a cream perforated paper edge and optional fine painted keyline. Keep its outer edge about 4% of the canvas short side from every canvas edge. The scene is the dominant visual event. Preserve source orientation relationships through recomposition, never stretch or crop away the key subject.

Use minimal information density, subtle ornament and a bottom-band title as the default. A short hero-overlay title is allowed only when the scene has clear negative space. Use exactly one title mode and one shared alignment axis for the title group. Avoid elaborate borders, repeated stamps, floating labels and multiple frames. The host app supplies editable message, date and postage, so omit denomination, cancellation mark, serial number, issue year and official postal claims in this adaptation.

## Typography and truth

Use one short title of 2–5 words, or 2–6 Chinese characters when the user supplies Chinese text. Match a supplied exact title; otherwise choose a quiet scene-derived poetic title. Place it in the paper band, in a refined serif (Song for Chinese), with the darkest scene ink and generous breathing room. Do not add a second paragraph, fake handwriting, decorative gibberish or duplicate translations. Do not invent countries, coordinates, historical facts or personal signatures. A supplied verified location can replace the poetic title. Existing meaningful signs remain part of the scene and are not rewritten as invented brands.

## Visual check

One recognizable scene; true watercolor repaint; coherent paper and perforation; readable short title; aligned margins; no fabricated metadata; no extra postcard back, device mockup, desktop props or comparison panel.
