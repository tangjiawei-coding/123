# style · watercolor（水彩手绘风）

> 手绘水彩晕染，旅行手帐质感。风景绝配：天空、水面、山色、街角都出彩。

## `$STYLE_ART`（中央主图画法）

Hand-painted watercolor illustration stamp art. Soft transparent washes, wet-on-wet blending, gentle color bleeds and blooms, visible brush strokes, delicate pencil or fine ink line accents outlining key subjects. Warm, painterly, travel-journal feel painted on textured watercolor paper. Luminous and airy.

## `$FRAME`（外框）

Soft elegant stamp frame: ivory/cream perforated border, slightly soft edge, optional thin painted keyline or a faint watercolor wash border. Denomination in an elegant corner mark.

## `$FINISH`（印刷质感）

Slightly textured neutral-tone watercolor paper print, visible fine paper fibers, soft matte, gentle and tactile. Carry subtle ink-density variation and watercolor absorption grain through the illustrated scene, with minimal rubbing at perforations and keylines. Preserve the original palette: no automatic yellowing, sepia wash, brown cast, grime, or heavy antique aging.

## `$TYPE`（字体倾向）

Elegant serif throughout for warmth. Use a refined script-compatible face by `$DISPLAY_LANGUAGE`: Song 宋体 for `zh`, Mincho 明朝体 for `ja`, Myeongjo 명조 for `ko`, and a Garamond/Caslon-like Latin face for `en`/`fr` plus English fallback. Title in a slightly hand-drawn or classic serif; country and subtitle smaller, italic or letter-spaced where the script supports it. Optional subtle hand-lettered feel for the locale's tagline. Keep added strings in the destination locale and do not repeat English or add Chinese to foreign destinations. Graceful, literary, warm.

## 适配 / 不适配

- ✅ 自然风景、天空/水面/山、街景、古镇、植物花卉
- ⚠️ 强几何/硬线条现代建筑 → 水彩会柔化轮廓，想要更现代硬朗的印刷感选 risograph
